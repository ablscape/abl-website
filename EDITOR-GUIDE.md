# Updating the website with Claude

You change the website by telling Claude what you want. Claude makes the change, shows you a preview, and publishes it when you say so. You never edit a file and never open Netlify.

## Each time

1. Open Claude and choose the **abl-website** repository.
2. Say what you want, and attach any photographs.
3. Claude replies with a preview link. Open it on your phone and on your computer.
4. Tell Claude what to fix, or say **publish**.

Nothing reaches the live site until you say publish.

## Things you can ask for

- "Add a project. The photographs are attached. It is the Lime Kiln Theater, finished in 2025, for Lime Kiln Arts. Here is what we did and why: ..."
- "On Courthouse Square, replace the second paragraph with this: ..."
- "Swap the first photograph on the VMI page for the one attached."
- "Put Courthouse Square first on the home page."
- "Add Bugs's biography to the People page: ..."
- "On the Process page, change the planting plan description to: ..."
- "Change the phone number to ..."
- "Hide the Thornhill project for now."
- "What is still unfinished on the site?"
- "Undo the change I published this morning."

## What helps Claude get it right

- **Give the facts.** Client, year, the firm's role, who else worked on it. Claude will not guess them. It will ask, or leave a yellow "To come" note in the preview.
- **Tell the story in four parts** when you can: the problem, what the firm studied, what it designed, what resulted. Rough notes are fine. Claude will tidy them and mark the text for your review.
- **Send the largest photograph files you have.** At least 2560 pixels wide is the target. Say who took each one if it was not the firm.
- **Say whether a private client has agreed** to be named or shown.

## Yellow notes and drafts

- Yellow notes in a preview mark text or photographs still to come. They never appear on the live site.
- A project marked "Draft" appears in previews only. Tell Claude "publish the Courthouse Square project" when its text and facts are final.

## If something looks wrong

Tell Claude what you see: "the photograph is sideways," "the second paragraph is missing." If Claude cannot fix it, ask Thomas.
